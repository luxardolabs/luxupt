# 2026.08.0 — 2026-09-08

Build timelapses from your NVR's own recordings, plus fixes to camera links, timelapse jobs and settings pages that were quietly not working.

## NEW

- **Timelapses from Protect recordings** `Timelapses` — Build a timelapse for a day you never had live capture running, straight from the recordings already on your NVR. Pick a date range and get either one video per day or a single combined video across the whole range.
- **Choose where frames come from** `Scheduler` — The nightly scheduler can use live captures or Protect recordings. Options you have not configured are shown as unavailable, with what is missing, instead of failing later.
- **Capture statistics over time** `Cameras` — Charts for capture performance with back/forward navigation through previous periods and a jump back to the current one.

## IMPROVED

- **Settings pages look and behave consistently** `Cameras · Scheduler · System` — Fields, toggles and buttons across the settings panels are now the same components, so spacing, focus rings and keyboard behaviour match everywhere. Buttons that act on a row now carry proper labels for screen readers.
- **Recording times shown in your timezone** `Timelapses` — The available recording range now displays in the timezone of the browser you are viewing from, rather than the server's.
- **Times are stored properly and still shown in your local time** `Everywhere` — Timestamps are now recorded in a way that does not depend on the server's timezone, while everything you see stays in local time exactly as before. Existing history is converted as part of the upgrade, so nothing shifts on screen.
- **Clearer unavailable states** `Scheduler` — A disabled option explains why it is disabled ("set a camera API key…") rather than just appearing greyed out.

## FIXED

- **"View Images" and "Timelapses" on a camera returned an empty list** `Cameras` — Both links filtered by the wrong camera identifier and always came back with nothing. The dropdown filters on those pages were unaffected.
- **A timelapse job could silently disappear** `Timelapses` — A queued job could be dropped mid-encode with nothing in the logs. Jobs are now held for their whole run.
- **Timelapses could be re-encoded unnecessarily** `Timelapses` — The check for "this timelapse already exists" never matched, so work that was already done could run again.
- **Some older timelapses were missing from the browser** `Timelapses` — 13 timelapses existed on disk and in the database but could not be reached through any camera filter. They now appear.
- **A capture late in the evening could be labelled "Tomorrow"** `Images · Timelapses` — The Today / Yesterday labels were worked out from the wrong day boundary.
- **Video exports made the rest of the app stutter** `Timelapses` — Downloading a recording from the NVR held up other requests for the length of the download.
- **Some failures disappeared without a trace** `System` — Five clean-up steps threw their errors away, so a failure looked identical to success. They now log what went wrong.
- **Several screens could fail to load entirely** `Cameras · Images · Timelapses · System` — The camera panel, the image filter bar, the About page, the job progress panel and every "save failed" message returned an error page instead of rendering. Each is fixed, and every page in the app is now loaded by an automated check on each build so this class cannot return.
- **Saving the scheduler could fail** `Scheduler` — Saving from a form that did not include the encoding options failed outright.
- **The About page showed blank values** `System` — Three settings on the About page rendered as empty rather than showing their values.
- **Backup and user forms did not always close after saving** `System` — The confirmation panel now closes as intended.
